Experience or story?

Present perfect = experience
No time. It opens the topic.
I’ve tried sushi. Have you ever eaten stew? I haven’t had lunch yet.
have / has + past participle
Past simple = story
Finished time. It gives the details.
I tried sushi in Tokyo last year. It was amazing.
verb + -ed / irregular

Past simple

7 min. Rule of thumb: the present perfect opens (Have you ever…?), the past simple gives details (When? Where? Did you like it?). Link back to her no. 1: “I got a chinchilla when I was ten” has a finished time. CCQs: I’ve tried sushi: Do we know when? (No) · I tried sushi last year: Is “last year” finished? (Yes). Neat trap: “How long have you…” is not needed today.

Choose the tense

5 min, controlled. After each choice she reads the whole sentence aloud. Ask: “Is there a finished time? Do we know when?” Signal words: since, yet, ever, last Friday, an hour ago.
